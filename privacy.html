Privacy policy

EFFECTIVE 4 SEPTEMBER 2026 · CLOSE FLOW AI LLC · TEXAS

This is a short policy because we collect very little. Close Flow AI LLC ("we", "us") runs this website and the services described on it. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

  • What you type into the form — your name, business, email, phone, and whatever you write in the message box. That's it. There is no account, no login, no tracking pixel on this site.
  • Booking details if you book a call through the calendar link — handled by Google Calendar under Google's own privacy terms.
  • Basic server logs from our hosting provider (Netlify), which may include your IP address and browser type for security and uptime purposes.

Why we collect it

To reply to your request and, if we work together, to do the work. If you send the form, you're agreeing that we may contact you about it by email or phone. We reply personally; there is no automated drip campaign attached to this form.

Email and text messages

Any email we send includes a way to opt out, and one word back ("stop", "not for me") ends it. We do not send marketing text messages to anyone who hasn't asked for them. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. If you become a client and your customers receive text follow-ups through a system we run for you, those messages go only to people who submitted a request to your business, and every message includes a way to opt out. Message and data rates may apply; message frequency varies.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent, or trade your information.
  • We don't share it with anyone except the providers needed to run the service (hosting, email, calendar, messaging) — and only what they need to run it.
  • We don't use advertising cookies on this site.

How long we keep it

Form submissions are kept as long as we're in a conversation with you, or as long as we work together, plus what the law requires for business records. Ask and we'll delete what we hold about you.

Your choices

Email closeflowscales@gmail.com to see, correct, or delete what we have about you, or to opt out of any contact. We answer these ourselves, usually the same day.

Client data

If you're a client, the leads, customer lists, and account data you give us stay yours. We use them only to do the work you hired us for, we keep access limited to the people doing that work, and we hand everything back or delete it when you ask.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions: closeflowscales@gmail.com.
